Topics our students are excited to explore

We welcome speakers from many areas of finance. A session can be practical, career-focused, or simply a conversation about what your work is really like.

Personal finance

Budgeting, credit, saving, taxes, college costs, financial planning, and smart money habits.

Markets & investing

Stocks, bonds, ETFs, derivatives, market structure, portfolio thinking, and risk management.

Careers & technology

Banking, asset management, fintech, accounting, audit, data, AI, cybersecurity, compliance, and more.
